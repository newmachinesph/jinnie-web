## Nuances recorded in the facts sheet

- NCR-27's injunction is contested (the ₱10 billion bond was never posted) and the case is before the Supreme Court; the increase was still described as suspended in early September 2026.
- AFASA holds run 5 days, extendable to 30 in total without a court order (Circular 1215).
- Basic necessities and prime commodities carry a separate 5% senior discount, no VAT exemption, capped at ₱1,300 a week (RA 7581 as amended), not the 20% under RA 9994.
- A bill raising the income tax exemption to ₱350,000 cleared the House committee in August 2026; ₱250,000 remains the law.
