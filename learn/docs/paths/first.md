# First money (18 to 24)

The flagship path. For the first years of earning: a first payslip, a first card, a first emergency, a first padala. Every number starts from a ₱20,000 Metro Manila salary and follows it through a year; Jinnie substitutes the learner's own figures wherever it has them. Fully authored: nine lessons, 48 bites. Verbatim voice lines, illustration ids and data hooks are generated into `docs/manifest/first.md`.

## Lesson 1: Sweldo
Objectives: read gross, deductions and net; know what SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and withholding tax buy; compute 13th month; know the pay floors the Labor Code sets.
Bites: sweldo.1 number (SSS on ₱20,000), sweldo.2 match (deductions to benefits), sweldo.3 predict (income tax on ₱20,000 is ₱0), sweldo.4 number (13th month, March to December), sweldo.5 rapid (rights on the payslip), sweldo.6 story (₱18,000 with benefits versus ₱20,000 cash).
Data hooks: wallet (open), check-benefits commitment, thirteenth wish (goals:write).
Facts to verify: overtime 25%, night differential 10%, service incentive leave 5 days, regular holiday 200%, payslip requirement under DOLE rules.

## Lesson 2: Budget
Objectives: sort needs from wants; split net pay into four jars; carry a daily number; price convenience.
Bites: budget.1 swipe (need or want), budget.2 allocate (four jars on ₱18,300), budget.3 number (daily number ₱307), budget.4 compare (tingi versus jar), budget.5 story (three paydays).
Data hooks: budget (open), record-7 commitment (wallet:read), fund-30 commitment (goals:read).
Facts to verify: sachet and jar prices are illustrative.

## Lesson 3: Ipon
Objectives: size a three-month emergency fund from needs, not salary; place it in an insured, separate, same-day account; automate the payday transfer.
Bites: ipon.1 predict (30% could weather an emergency, BSP 2025), ipon.2 number (₱27,450), ipon.3 choice (where to keep it), ipon.4 order (five setup steps), ipon.5 story (the flat tire month).
Data hooks: emergency wish (goals:write), accounts (open), fund-30 commitment.
Facts to verify: PDIC ₱1,000,000; lending app 15% a month used as the cap-level example.

## Lesson 4: Utang
Objectives: see what a minimum payment costs; convert any loan to a yearly rate; compare a fee to an interest rate; treat installments as debt; read a credit report.
Bites: utang.1 predict (31 months at ₱500 on ₱10,000), utang.2 number (5-6 is 20% a cycle), utang.3 compare (pawnshop versus lending app), utang.4 swipe (buy now pay later true or false), utang.5 order (credit report steps), utang.6 story (the gadget on installment).
Data hooks: credit (open), debts (open).
Facts to verify: 3% card cap, ₱4 per ₱100 pawn rate, the ₱750 app fee example, CIC dispute process.

## Lesson 5: Proteksyon
Objectives: know PhilHealth pays a fixed amount per condition; name the six SSS benefits; choose term over VUL for cover; understand how an HMO stacks; know microinsurance exists.
Bites: proteksyon.1 predict (PhilHealth about 56%), proteksyon.2 match (SSS benefits), proteksyon.3 compare (term versus VUL, illustrative), proteksyon.4 choice (HMO order of payment), proteksyon.5 rapid (protection facts), proteksyon.6 story (the admission: accredited hospital, PhilHealth first).
Data hooks: check-benefits commitment.
Facts to verify: support value 56% (PIDS), maternity 105 days, unemployment 36 contributions, term and VUL premiums illustrative, microinsurance under ₱50 a month.

## Lesson 6: Scam defense
Objectives: recognise the fear-link-code shape; never share a one-time code; sort investment red flags; act in the first hour; verify family requests by voice.
Bites: scam.1 spot (fake wallet deactivation SMS), scam.2 choice (the caller who needs the code), scam.3 swipe (investment red flags), scam.4 order (first hour after a takeover), scam.5 story (the cousin's new account).
Mock screenshots: scam.1, an SMS styled as a wallet alert with a fake domain.
Data hooks: drill enrolment, accounts (open) for hotlines.
Facts to verify: AFASA hold mechanics, OTP retirement scope, TransUnion 72% targeted.

## Lesson 7: Invest
Objectives: place products on the risk ladder; know MP2 terms; know what a retail treasury bond pays and when; separate investing from speculation.
Bites: invest.1 order (risk ladder), invest.2 predict (MP2, ₱120,000 becomes about ₱143,000), invest.3 choice (bond mechanics), invest.4 rapid (stocks, funds, crypto), invest.5 story (the tip in the group chat).
Data hooks: mp2 wish (goals:write).
Facts to verify: MP2 7.12% for 2025 and the compounding illustration, RTB-31 figures, 20% tax on bond interest, 10% dividend tax, 3.64 million PSE accounts.

## Lesson 8: Retirement basics
Objectives: know the average SSS pension and the 120-contribution rule; tell Pension Booster from PERA; compute the PERA tax credit; feel the cost of a ten-year delay.
Bites: retire.1 predict (average pension under ₱5,000), retire.2 match (Pension Booster, PERA, regular SSS), retire.3 number (₱1,200 credit on ₱24,000), retire.4 predict (₱690,000 versus ₱1,420,000), retire.5 story (the employer match).
Data hooks: retire wish (goals:write).
Facts to verify: PERA 5% credit and caps, employer 150% deduction, Pension Booster minimum, the 6% growth illustration.

## Lesson 9: Pamilya money
Objectives: send money home at the lowest cost; treat the lista as credit to clear first; know paluwagan, lista, 5-6, padala, sangla; size the padala jar; say yes to one thing a month.
Bites: pamilya.1 compare (wallet versus remittance counter, illustrative), pamilya.2 choice (paying the lista), pamilya.3 match (informal money vocabulary), pamilya.4 allocate (jars with padala), pamilya.5 story (padala month).
Data hooks: remit (open), circles (open), fund-30 commitment.
Facts to verify: cash-out and remittance fees illustrative.
