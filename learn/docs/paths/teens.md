# Baon (13 to 17)
For high-school students managing a weekly allowance, a phone, and a Messenger inbox for the first time. It teaches baon as a real budget and DMs as a place that asks for money in disguise, under a family gate with a parent kept in view.

## Lesson 1: The baon budget
Objectives:
- Turn a weekly baon into a daily number that survives all five school days.
- Sort canteen spending into needs and wants without cutting either one out.
- See a small daily spend and an unplanned midweek loan at their real, multiplied size.

Bites:
- `teens.allowance.1` — number — compute the daily number from a weekly baon.
- `teens.allowance.2` — swipe — sort canteen items into need or want.
- `teens.allowance.3` — predict — guess the monthly cost of a small daily snack.
- `teens.allowance.4` — allocate — split the week's baon into four jars.
- `teens.allowance.5` — story — lend from pocket cash or from the savings jar when a classmate asks to borrow.

## Lesson 2: Scams in your DMs
Objectives:
- Recognize the giveaway, top-up, and "send load first" patterns that ask for money or an OTP before anything is proven.
- Tell a real friend's message from a hacked account's message, and know to verify by calling before sending anything.
- Know the five-minute response when a scam is spotted, and that reporting is available on the platform and to a parent.

Bites:
- `teens.dms.1` — choice — respond to a fake GCash raffle giveaway message.
- `teens.dms.2` — swipe — sort game top-up offers into trustworthy or a warning sign.
- `teens.dms.3` — spot — tap the suspicious lines in a hacked friend's chat.
- `teens.dms.4` — order — sequence the first five minutes after spotting a scam.
- `teens.dms.5` — rapid — true or false on DM safety and the law that covers account scams.
- `teens.dms.6` — story — a late-night "send load" message from a cousin's account.

Voice lines to record:
- `teens.dms.1` hook — en: "A message says you are one of 50 winners chosen for a ₱1,000 GCash raffle. You do not remember joining any raffle." / tl: "May mensaheng nagsasabing isa ka sa 50 nanalo ng ₱1,000 GCash raffle. Hindi mo naaalala na sumali ka sa kahit anong raffle."
- `teens.dms.1` show — en: "A real giveaway can name the exact post you joined, and never asks for your PIN, OTP, or a processing fee first. A message that skips straight to asking for those is not checking if you won. It is checking if you will pay." / tl: "Kayang sabihin ng tunay na giveaway kung anong post mo sinalihan, at hindi ito humihingi ng PIN, OTP, o processing fee muna. Ang mensaheng diretsong humihingi nito ay hindi tumitingin kung nanalo ka. Tinitingnan nito kung magbabayad ka."
- `teens.dms.1` keep — en: "A prize that needs your OTP first is not a prize." / tl: "Ang premyong kailangan muna ng OTP mo ay hindi premyo."
- `teens.dms.2` hook — en: "Game top-up pages, resellers, and official-looking accounts fill your feed before every big update. A few of them are not selling anything." / tl: "Puno ng game top-up page, reseller, at mukhang-official na account ang feed mo bago ang bawat malaking update. May ilan sa kanila na wala talagang binebenta."
- `teens.dms.2` show — en: "A real seller shows a receipt after payment, and the diamonds land in the game itself. A set-up asks for payment first, to a personal account, then goes quiet." / tl: "Ang tunay na seller ay may ibinibigay na resibo pagkatapos magbayad, at dumarating ang diamonds sa mismong laro. Ang patibong ay humihingi ng bayad muna, sa personal na account, tapos biglang nawawala."
- `teens.dms.2` keep — en: "Payment before the item, to a personal account, is the pattern to remember." / tl: "Bayad bago ang item, sa personal account, ang pattern na dapat tandaan."
- `teens.dms.3` hook — en: "Twenty seconds. A message arrives from a friend you actually know. Something about it does not match how she usually types." / tl: "Dalawampung segundo. May dumating na mensahe mula sa kaibigan mong kilala mo talaga. May hindi bagay sa karaniwan niyang pag-type."
- `teens.dms.3` show — en: "An account can be taken over without its owner knowing. It still shows the real name and photo. What gives it away is the request, and the way it is written." / tl: "Puwedeng ma-take over ang account kahit hindi alam ng may-ari. Nananatili ang tunay na pangalan at larawan. Ang request at paraan ng pagsulat ang nagbubunyag."
- `teens.dms.3` keep — en: "Before sending anything, call. A voice is harder to fake than a chat." / tl: "Bago magpadala ng anuman, tumawag. Mas mahirap peke ang boses kaysa sa chat."
- `teens.dms.4` hook — en: "You just spotted a scam message, or a friend's account is acting strange. The next five minutes matter more than being right about it." / tl: "Nakakita ka lang ng mensaheng scam, o kakaiba ang kilos ng account ng kaibigan mo. Mas mahalaga ang susunod na limang minuto kaysa sa pagiging tama tungkol dito."
- `teens.dms.4` show — en: "A clear order stops the situation from spreading to your account, or to your friend's." / tl: "Ang malinaw na pagkakasunod ang pumipigil sa paglaganap ng sitwasyon sa account mo, o sa account ng kaibigan mo."
- `teens.dms.4` keep — en: "You are allowed to double-check a friend. A real one will understand why." / tl: "Puwede mong i-double check ang kaibigan. Mauunawaan ito ng tunay na kaibigan."
- `teens.dms.5` hook — en: "Ninety seconds. Ten statements about staying safe on Messenger and Telegram." / tl: "Siyamnapung segundo. Sampung pahayag tungkol sa pananatiling ligtas sa Messenger at Telegram."
- `teens.dms.5` show — en: "A law now exists specifically for account scams like these. Knowing the basics costs nothing and saves everything." / tl: "May batas na ngayon na tukoy sa mga account scam na ganito. Walang bayad ang pag-alam ng basics, at nakakaligtas ito ng lahat."
- `teens.dms.5` keep — en: "None of these facts help if they are not used in the moment. That is why you just practiced." / tl: "Walang tutulong ang mga factong ito kung hindi gagamitin sa mismong sandali. Kaya ka nag-practice."
- `teens.dms.6` hook — en: "It is 9 in the evening. A message from your cousin's account arrives, asking for a small favor." / tl: "Alas nuwebe ng gabi. May dumating na mensahe mula sa account ng pinsan mo, humihingi ng maliit na pabor."
- `teens.dms.6` show — en: "Everything from this lesson meets one message. What you do in the next minute is the whole lesson." / tl: "Lahat ng natutunan sa araling ito ay makakaharap sa isang mensahe. Ang gagawin mo sa susunod na isang minuto ang buong aralin."
- `teens.dms.6` keep — en: "A call takes one minute. A wrong reply cannot be undone." / tl: "Isang minuto lang ang tawag. Hindi na mababawi ang maling reply."
