For children who get baon and see money change hands at the canteen and the sari-sari store. Family gate: a parent reads alongside, and the only actions a bite can propose are none at all or a nudge to open Circles with a parent.

## Lesson 1: Three jars
Objectives:
- Split baon into three jars — spend, save, share — instead of letting it disappear with no plan.
- Name what each jar is for and divide a savings goal into weeks.
- Practise using the share jar to help a friend without touching money saved for a goal.

Bites:
- `kids.jars.1`, choice — Deciding how to split ₱50 of baon between spending today and setting money aside.
- `kids.jars.2`, allocate — Splitting ₱50 into spend, save, and share jars against guide percentage ranges.
- `kids.jars.3`, match — Matching each jar to what it is for.
- `kids.jars.4`, number — Dividing a ₱150 toy goal by ₱15 saved a week to find the number of weeks.
- `kids.jars.5`, story — Helping a friend, Louie, from the share jar without touching ₱120 saved for a jump rope.

## Lesson 2: Need or want
Objectives:
- Tell a need from a want among canteen items and count change correctly.
- Run four quick checks before buying a want.
- Decide how to spend what is left after money goes missing, prioritising the need.

Bites:
- `kids.canteen.1`, swipe — Sorting six canteen items into need and want.
- `kids.canteen.2`, predict — Guessing the change from a ₱20 payment on a ₱15 rice meal.
- `kids.canteen.3`, order — Sequencing four checks to run before buying a want.
- `kids.canteen.4`, rapid — Six true-or-false calls on need versus want.
- `kids.canteen.5`, story — Deciding how to spend the ₱50 left in your pocket after ₱50 goes missing.

Voice lines to record (verbatim, en / tl):

`kids.canteen.1` hook
- en: "The canteen line has rice meals and it has ice candy. Both cost money. Only one keeps you full until 3pm."
- tl: "May rice meal at may ice candy sa canteen. Pareho itong may bayad. Isa lang ang nagpapabusog sa iyo hanggang 3pm."
